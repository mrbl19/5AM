My Proudest Culinary Accomplishment


When I decided to open 5AM, I was set on creating a corn cookie. It was going to taste distinctly of corn, and it would be so good that it would imprint a memory on whoever tasted it.

Yes, it was a tall (i.e., unreasonable) order, but it was a nearly perfect idea. Corn cookies aren't something you typically see on a menu and it was going to be offered in a prime environment—a farmers market in central Illinois. So naturally, I wanted to execute it flawlessly.

Sadly, I never nailed it. Turns out, water-soluble flavors are very difficult to capture in baked goods. Plus other reasons which I plan to outline in other post. (But really in the grand scheme of it all, it was me. I got in my way. Specifically, my high expectations and I got in the way. And while I do plan to learn from it. I don't plan to blog on it.) Regardless, this recipe I developed almost makes up for the fact I didn't (or haven't 😉).

I've received overwhelming praise from people who have tasted this bun. And not just from friends, but from people who've purchased it at the market. And while my interpersonal skills aren't the greatest, I have a real knack for recognizing when people are being geninue. So trust me when I tell you these were geninue praises. (Notice how I am not saying compliments.)

This recipe is the product of wanting to create something cheese-esque, but shelf-stable at room temperature. The result is something that, according to those who've tried it, "tastes like nothing I've had before" but is also "so good."
